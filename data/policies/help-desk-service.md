# Help desk service levels

Urgent cases (a locked account, a document expiring within 48 hours, or a customer unable to pay a fine) receive a reply within 4 working hours.
Normal cases receive a reply within 2 working days.
Agents must reply in the customer's language: Arabic or English.
If a question is outside UAE government services, the agent politely says the help desk cannot help with it.
