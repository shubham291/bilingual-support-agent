# VAT registration and TRN

Businesses register for VAT on the EmaraTax portal of the Federal Tax Authority (FTA).
After registration, the FTA issues a Tax Registration Number (TRN) with 15 digits.
The TRN must appear on every tax invoice.
The standard VAT rate in the UAE is 5%.
