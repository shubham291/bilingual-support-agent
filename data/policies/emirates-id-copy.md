# Emirates ID renewal

Residents renew their Emirates ID through the ICP app or the ICP website.
Steps: log in, choose Emirates ID renewal, upload a passport copy and a recent photo, and pay the renewal fee.
A valid residence visa is required before the Emirates ID can be renewed.
The new card is delivered by courier to the address on the application.
Exact fees depend on the card's validity period and are shown on the ICP website before payment.
