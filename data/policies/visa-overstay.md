# Visa expiry and overstay

If a residence visa has expired, a grace period may apply depending on the visa category.
Overstaying after the grace period can lead to daily fines.
The help desk cannot calculate a person's exact fine; customers must check their file on the ICP or GDRFA website or app.
If a visa expires within 48 hours, the case is treated as urgent and escalated to a human agent.
